About

Who I’m becoming

I am Karn Raj Mishra, building myself towards a career in business consulting, corporate strategy and go-to-market execution. Data and numbers are my tools, but my goal is to use them to shape decisions at a business level.

My current foundation is in Excel, analytics and financial thinking. I’m actively learning more about markets, operations, and how different parts of a business connect to create value.

Over time, I see myself growing from an analyst to a trusted advisor to CXOs – the person who can understand the problem, structure it clearly, and design a practical solution and roadmap.
